The own-AI boost

We can't just say "gemma4's posts got the most upvotes", because that could mean gemma4 simply writes better posts. So we compare the same posts through both AIs' eyes, in two steps:

    This two-step subtraction is called a double difference. It cancels out "one AI writes better posts" (both groups read the same posts) and "one AI just likes everything" (we compare inside each group first). What's left is favouritism.
