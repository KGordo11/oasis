LLM Bias V3 Data

Every number from the V3 big sim, world by world: one reading AI reacting to the posts one AI's users wrote (gemma reading gemma's posts, gemma reading llama's, and so on). Nothing is added up into totals. The main comparison is V3 vs V3a: the same posts and users, with the action list in a new order. You can also compare V1 with V3: V1 used V1's order too, so it is compared with V3 (not V3a). 00 · How to read the numbers

Percentages first, differences when you ask

  • Two AIs in every number. The reading AI is the AI playing the 100 users who read and react. Posts written by is the AI whose users wrote the posts being read. "gemma reads llama's posts" = gemma plays the readers, the posts came from llama's posting turn. Every table says both.
  • % of posts read: out of every post a user read, how often the AI did this. "Upvoted 60%" means 60 of every 100 posts read got an upvote. A post read is one user reading one post.
  • Actions per 100 posts read (total engagement, the main measure): count every action except do_nothing, divide by posts read, times 100. It can be above 100, because one answer can hold several actions.
  • Points: switch "Show" to Difference and each pair of percentages becomes one number: the second run minus the first, in points. 60% → 50% is −10 points; 20% → 26% is +6 points. This is what "+2" or "−4" meant in earlier tables.
  • The own-AI favouritism number (section 11) is built from four percentages; that section shows all four and how they are combined.

02 · From raw files to clean data

Clean, normalize, aggregate: done the same way for every run

  1. Clean

    Kept out of the main tables, and counted: the noise-floor rereads, answers that were still unusable after 3 tries, and extra screens (profiles, searches, inbox), which have their own measure. A post that came back after a refresh counts as one post read, at the tick of its first look. Every action is marked "worked" or "failed", with why.

  2. Normalize

    Divide by posts read, because the piles are different sizes (gemma 99, llama 119, qwen 102 posts, each read by 98-99 users).

  3. Organize, small to big, always world by world

    Below, in this order, every table split by reading AI and by whose posts:

    each action→kind of answer→by feeling→each tick→each post→each user→each world

  4. Check

    Every V3 and V3a number was counted a second time straight from the raw files: 62,633 numbers, 0 differences. This page's world numbers are checked against the cleaned files when it is built.

11 · Own-AI favouritism

Does an AI give its own AI's posts more?

For two AIs, say gemma and llama, four percentages:

  1. gemma reading gemma's posts, and llama reading gemma's posts. The gap = how much more gemma gives gemma's posts than llama does.
  2. gemma reading llama's posts, and llama reading llama's posts. The gap = how much more gemma gives llama's posts than llama does. This is just how much more gemma upvotes everything.
  3. Favouritism = gap 1 − gap 2. Above 0: both AIs lean toward their own AI's posts. Below 0: they lean toward the other's. The range is where it lands 95% of the time when posts and users are resampled; CLEAR = the range does not include 0.

12 · Easy vs hard actions

Do the AIs pick the easy actions?

Each action gets a level from what it asks of the AI: 1 one click (upvote), 2 copy an ID from the screen (like a comment), 3 write text (comment), 4 write a whole post, 5 go to another screen first and then write (a private message).
