6. IS THE RECENCY EFFECT ITSELF JUST A ROUND EFFECT?
   A post can only be old in a late round, so post age is entangled
   with round, and engagement varies by round. Stratifying on the

7. THE MECHANISM: IT IS NOT AGE, IT IS REPEAT EXPOSURE
   A post can only be old and still in a feed if it has been
   circulating, which means the agent has probably already seen it.
   Age and prior sightings are therefore almost the same variable.
   `prior` counts earlier sightings of this (agent, post) across the
   whole feed, not just this tier.

  CAVEAT, and it is a real one: prior sightings are not randomly
  assigned. A post is re-shown because the ranker kept choosing it,
  so repeat-exposure status is an outcome of the same system whose
  effect is being estimated. Feed stratification fixes the agent,
  the round and the run, but not selection into being re-shown.
  The dose-response and the network-tier replication are what make
  the reading credible; neither makes it experimental.
